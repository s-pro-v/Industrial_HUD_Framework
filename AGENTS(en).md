# Instructions for AI assistants (Agents)

## Localization

Documentation and AI instructions are available in two languages:

- **Polish (PL):** [pl/AGENTS.md](../pl/AGENTS.md), [pl/README.md](../pl/README.md), [pl/cursorrules.md](../pl/cursorrules.md)
